# FollowTrain User Guide

Welcome to FollowTrain! This guide will help you understand what FollowTrain is and how to use it to coordinate group following on social media platforms.

## What is FollowTrain?

FollowTrain is a simple yet powerful tool that helps groups of people follow the same social media accounts together. Think of it as a "train" where everyone follows the same accounts in a coordinated way.

### Why Use FollowTrain?

- **Coordinate Group Following**: Perfect for marketing teams, book clubs, fan groups, or any community that wants to follow the same accounts
- **Cross-Platform Support**: Works with Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, and Telegram
- **Easy to Use**: No technical knowledge required
- **Real-Time Tracking**: See who has joined the train and when
- **No Installation Needed**: Works directly in your web browser

## How to Use FollowTrain

### Creating a FollowTrain

1. **Visit the Website**: Go to [https://followtrain.zeabur.app](https://followtrain.zeabur.app)
2. **Choose a Platform**: Select which social media platform you want to create a train for
3. **Enter Your Profile**: Paste your profile URL or username for that platform
4. **Name Your Train**: Give your train a descriptive name (optional)
5. **Create the Train**: Click "Create FollowTrain"
6. **Share the Link**: Copy the unique train link and share it with others

### Joining an Existing FollowTrain

1. **Receive the Link**: Get the FollowTrain link from someone who created it
2. **Open the Link**: Click on the link or paste it in your browser
3. **Enter Your Profile**: Paste your profile URL or username for the same platform
4. **Join the Train**: Click "Join Train"
5. **Follow Accounts**: Manually follow the accounts as directed by your group

## Supported Platforms

FollowTrain works with these popular social media platforms:

- **Instagram**: Share fashion, art, or lifestyle accounts
- **Twitter/X**: Follow news, influencers, or industry experts
- **LinkedIn**: Connect with professionals in your field
- **TikTok**: Discover trending creators and content
- **Facebook**: Follow community pages or interest groups
- **Telegram**: Join channels and groups

## Best Practices

### For Train Creators
- Give your train a clear, descriptive name
- Share the train link in a place where all participants can access it
- Consider setting expectations about which accounts to follow
- Encourage participants to join within a specific timeframe

### For Train Participants
- Make sure you're using the correct profile URL or username
- Join the train before following accounts to be counted
- Follow the group's agreed-upon timeline for following accounts
- Check that your profile is visible to others in the train

## Privacy and Security

- FollowTrain only collects the minimum information needed to operate
- Your social media credentials are never stored or accessed by FollowTrain
- Profile information is only visible to other train participants
- Trains automatically expire after 7 days for privacy

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't Find My Profile**
- Double-check that you entered the correct username or URL
- Make sure the profile is public (private profiles can't be accessed)
- Some platforms may have restrictions on profile access

**Train Link Not Working**
- Verify the link was copied correctly
- Check if the train has expired (trains last 7 days)
- Contact the train creator to confirm the link

**Profile Already in Train**
- Each profile can only join a train once
- If you need to rejoin, contact the train creator
